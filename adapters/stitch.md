# Google Stitch adapter

Use Stitch for divergence, composition, screen systems, rapid UX exploration, and prototype flow.

`DESIGN.md` is the portable visual-system artifact. `PROJECT-DNA.md` remains the stronger source for product truth and constraints.

## Workflow

1. Bring in the brief, current screens, and brand/reference evidence.
2. Load or derive `PROJECT-DNA.md` and `DESIGN.md`.
3. State the business objective and user feeling—not only visual adjectives.
4. Generate multiple structurally different routes.
5. Critique each route with Particular before selecting one.
6. Converge on a system, not a single polished screen.
7. Prototype key flow/state changes.
8. Export to Figma/code/AI Studio only after direction is stable.

## Rules

- Do not accept the first generated screen.
- Do not treat theme changes as concept exploration.
- Do not let Stitch overwrite explicit facts, constraints, or Anti-DNA.
- Preserve responsive behavior, states, and content hierarchy during handoff.
