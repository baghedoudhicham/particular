# Critique Module

Use CRITIQUE to evaluate a concept, design, or production build without automatically redesigning it.

## Four critics

### Client critic
Would this stakeholder actually choose it based on observed preference evidence?

### User critic
Can the intended audience understand, trust, navigate, and act?

### Brand critic
Could this experience plausibly belong only to this organization?

### Engineering critic
Can it survive real implementation, content variability, accessibility, and performance constraints?

Each critic returns:

- 3 strongest decisions
- 3 weakest decisions
- 1 thing to remove
- 1 thing to refine
- 1 risk

## Gates

Check factual accuracy, client desirability, category fit, brand specificity, IA, content, composition, type, imagery, interaction, functional UX, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, search/discoverability, implementation, AI-slop, category-slop, transplant test, and realistic-size inspection.

Do not defend the design during critique. Diagnose first.
