# Discover Module

Use DISCOVER before design when the business, audience, category, constraints, or references are not yet sufficiently understood.

## Inputs

Collect:

- authoritative brief/specification
- brand assets and guidelines
- existing website/product
- reference sites/screenshots
- target audiences
- business model and conversion goals
- content sources
- CMS/data constraints
- implementation stack
- accessibility/performance requirements
- prior client feedback, ratings, selections, and rejections

## Output

Return:

1. source hierarchy
2. locked facts vs. unknowns
3. audience and user-intent map
4. business and conversion model
5. category definition
6. trust requirements and objections
7. content/evidence gaps
8. reference inventory
9. risks and contradictions
10. next recommended mode

Do not generate a style direction before evidence is sufficient.
