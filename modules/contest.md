# Contest Module

Use CONTEST when client ratings, selections, eliminations, or competing concepts provide preference evidence.

## Preference ledger

For each concept record:

- direction
- rating/status
- feedback
- survived/declined
- client comments
- visual characteristics
- content characteristics

Update Client DNA after every signal.

## Interpretation

Do not reduce rejection to “needs more polish.” Diagnose whether the problem was:

- visual category
- client desirability
- weak brand expression
- excessive cleverness
- insufficient convention/trust
- genericness
- poor proof model
- incorrect content hierarchy

## Evidence rule

**Observed selection > designer theory** for subjective preference.

Factual truth still outranks preference truth.

## Post-result delta

When a winner/selected design appears compare:

- what they chose
- what we built
- where they differ
- what the brief appeared to say
- what selection reveals
- which lesson is project-specific
- which lesson generalizes

Do not overfit a single contest result.
